I’ve worked extensively on bringing design standards into compliance with W3C/WCAG accessibility standards since 2017.

Case study - Brand refresh, redesign and development

Rebuilding Bohemian FC’s web presence

Founded in 1890, Bohemian FC are the oldest football club in continuous existence in Ireland. During the financial crisis, Bohemians almost went out of business and needed to rely on the goodwill of volunteers, supporters and community members to survive.

I completely rebuilt The Bohemian FC web presence on a voluntary basis, helping the club to now boast the highest commercial/merchandise income in the League of Ireland.
